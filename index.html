How it works

1

Bring your sales history

Export a report such as Total sales over time from Shopify Analytics, or an Orders export. You can also let Forecraft read your store's orders directly.

2

Pick a forecast method

Auto mode backtests several statistical models against your own data and uses the one that would have been most accurate.

3

Plan with confidence

See projected sales with a likely range, broken down by product, so you can plan inventory, budgets and promotions.

Real forecasting methods, explained

Linear trend + weekly seasonality

Finds the overall direction of your sales and the day-of-week pattern most stores have.

Holt-Winters & Holt's linear

Exponential smoothing models that give recent sales more weight and adapt as your trend shifts.

Show the math

Every forecast lists its fitted parameters and error estimates, so you know exactly how a number was made.

Your CSV stays on your computer

When you upload a report, Forecraft reads and forecasts it entirely in your browser. The file is never sent to our servers or to any third party. Read the privacy policy.
