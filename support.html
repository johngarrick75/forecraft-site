Frequently asked questions

Which report should I upload?

In Shopify admin, go to Analytics → Reports and open a report grouped by day, for example Total sales over time. Choose a date range of at least 60 days, then click Export → CSV. An Orders export (Orders → Export) also works and gives you per-product sales.

How much history do I need?

At least 60 days gives a usable forecast. Weekly seasonality is applied once there are 21 or more days of daily data, and more history generally means a steadier forecast.

Is my CSV uploaded anywhere?

No. An uploaded CSV is read and forecast entirely in your browser and never reaches our servers. See the privacy policy for details.

What does "Auto" do?

Auto backtests each forecast method on the most recent part of your history and uses the one that would have predicted it most accurately.

My large CSV is slow to load.

Big files take longer because everything is processed on your computer. Give it a moment. If it fails, try a shorter date range or a report grouped by day instead of an Orders export.

How do I upgrade, change or cancel my plan?

Open Forecraft in your Shopify admin and click Upgrade (or Plan) in the app menu, then View plans. Billing goes through Shopify, so charges appear on your Shopify invoice. Uninstalling the app also cancels your subscription.

How do I uninstall Forecraft?

In Shopify admin, go to Settings → Apps, find Forecraft, and choose Uninstall. Your app session data is deleted when you uninstall.
