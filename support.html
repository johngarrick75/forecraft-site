Frequently asked questions

Which report should I upload?

In Shopify admin, go to Analytics → Reports and open a report grouped by day, for example Total sales over time. Choose a date range of at least 60 days, then click Export → CSV. An Orders export (Orders → Export) also works and gives you per-product sales.

What can I forecast?

Sales only. From a CSV, any sales column in the report, such as total, net or gross sales; choose it under Metric to project after uploading. Orders, units sold, returns, discounts, shipping and taxes aren't forecast. With an Orders export, you can also forecast sales per product. When Forecraft reads your store's orders directly, it forecasts total sales and net sales per product. Projections cover the next 30, 60, 90 and 120 days, plus 180 and 365 days on the paid plan. When reading your store's orders, the Free plan analyzes the last 30 days; the paid plan can analyze up to 365 days.

How much history do I need?

Forecraft can forecast from as little as 7 days, but 60 days or more gives a much steadier forecast: Auto needs 60+ days to compare methods, and weekly seasonality is applied once there are 21 or more days of daily data. On the Free plan, store data uses the last 30 days; for longer history, upload a report CSV or upgrade to analyze up to 365 days of orders.

Is my CSV uploaded anywhere?

No. An uploaded CSV is read and forecast entirely in your browser and never reaches our servers. See the privacy policy for details.

What does "Auto" do?

Auto backtests each forecast method on the most recent part of your history and uses the one that would have predicted it most accurately. With less than 60 days of history there isn't enough to backtest, so Auto uses the linear trend method.

My large CSV is slow to load.

Big files take longer because everything is processed on your computer. Give it a moment. If it fails, try a shorter date range or a report grouped by day instead of an Orders export.

How do I upgrade, change or cancel my plan?

Open Forecraft in your Shopify admin and click Upgrade (or Plan) in the app menu, then View plans. Billing goes through Shopify, so charges appear on your Shopify invoice. Uninstalling the app also cancels your subscription.

How do I uninstall Forecraft?

In Shopify admin, go to Settings → Apps, find Forecraft, and choose Uninstall. Your app session data is deleted when you uninstall.
